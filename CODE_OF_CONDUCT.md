# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a welcoming, useful, and technically rigorous project. Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Acceptable participation includes constructive feedback, clear technical context, graceful correction, respect for privacy, and attention to accessibility. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private data are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers. On GitHub, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting or maintainer-contact channel when available. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the project. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
