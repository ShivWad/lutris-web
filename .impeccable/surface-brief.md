# Library browser surface

- Mode: Operate. Desktop-first, responsive web app for a Linux game owner with several Lutris-connected stores.
- Job: identify what is owned and where, narrow thousands of entries quickly, inspect normalized and raw metadata, and deliberately refresh the local index.
- Constraint: read-only. No install, launch, modification, deletion, arbitrary shell input, or fabricated provider claims.
- Direction: a dark archival contact sheet. Covers are indexed frames; sources are archive drawers; selected metadata is a persistent catalog ledger.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/library-approved.png`. Do not literalize its play/install actions, serif body face, or fictional filesystem data.
- Grammar: squared image frames; 12–14px control radii; 1px graphite rules; soft offset elevation only for active controls and lifted covers; Archivo display, Manrope body; brass registration marks; a single panel reveal motion.
- Primary action: `Refresh library`, semantic button with explicit busy and error states.
- Responsive: persistent rail and detail ledger become bottom navigation and full-screen record respectively; cover cells stay intact; controls scroll rather than compress illegibly.
